MazeQuest

Navigate the maze with your hands! Wave to move — no keyboard needed! 👋

👋 How to Play
🟢

Start

Touch START with index finger to draw

👆

Move

Guide green dot through path

⚠️

Avoid Walls

Hitting walls triggers restart

🏁

Finish

Reach END to win the game

📷 Camera Access Needed
MazeQuest uses your camera to see your hand movements. When you click Start Playing, your browser will ask for camera permission — just click Allow!
🔒 Your camera video never leaves your device.
